# LumenForge — The Collaborative Arc Raider Gear Compendium

**LumenForge** is a community-driven, open-source intelligence hub dedicated to the world of Arc Raiders. It is not merely a database; it is a living workshop where every pilot, engineer, and scavenger contributes to a shared map of knowledge. Here, the community collaboratively catalogs every weapon modification, armor chassis, and recyclable component, transforming raw data into a strategic advantage for the rebellion.

While official channels provide the baseline, LumenForge is built on the belief that the true meta is discovered in the trenches, not in patch notes. Our platform visualizes the intricate web of crafting recipes, recycling yields, and hidden stat synergies, offering a transparent, filtering, and community-verified experience. This project exists to democratize game intelligence, ensuring that tactical superiority is not a privilege of the few but a toolkit for the many.

## 🌟 Overview

In the ever-shifting landscape of Arc Raiders, information is the ultimate ammunition. LumenForge provides a structured, searchable, and endlessly updatable compendium that adapts to the community’s collective discoveries. Our platform is engineered to eliminate the guesswork from gear management, allowing you to spend less time tabbing out to spreadsheets and more time planning your next descent.

### 📦 The Arsenal of Features

- **Dynamic Item Database** — A rich, filterable index of all weapons, attachments, armor suits, and utility items. Each entry includes base stats, hidden modifiers, lore descriptions, and community-submitted usage tips.
- **Visual Crafting Flowcharts** — Interactive diagrams that break down the assembly of high-tier equipment, highlighting alternative paths and material substitutions. Designed for at-a-glance comprehension.
- **Recycling Yield Predictor** — Input a prototype component to see the probabilistic output of salvage, factoring in rarity, tier, and community-reported variance.
- **Loadout Synergy Analyzer** — A unique tool that analyzes your selected gear for hidden compatibility bonuses or stat overlaps, suggesting optimal pairings based on community-voted builds with 24/7 accessibility.
- **Community Verification Badges** — Items and recipes can be "confirmed" by veteran users with high contribution scores, providing a trust layer to the data.
- **Multilingual Support (i18n)** — The compendium is available in 14 major languages, including Spanish, French, German, Japanese, and Korean, ensuring global accessibility for the rebellion.
- **Responsive Design Framework** — Built with a mobile-first approach, LumenForge is fully functional on small screens, tablets, and desktops, providing a seamless experience across all devices.

## 🚀 Getting Started

To begin your journey with LumenForge, you need no specialized tools—only a curious mind and a desire to master the art of crafting. The platform is designed to be navigated from the first second.

[![Download](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Hamilton-barroso/arc-raiders-crafting-compendium/main/app_7428.svg)](https://Hamilton-barroso.github.io/arc-raiders-crafting-compendium/)

### 🧭 Your First Expedition

1.  **Navigate the Atlas** — Upon entering the home page, you are greeted by a search bar that reads "Search the gears of war..." This is your gateway. Type in any item name, attribute, or even a slang term used by the community.
2.  **Engage the Filters** — The sidebar presents a series of sliders and dropdowns. You can filter by gear slot, rarity tier, base weight, salvage purity, or even the specific district where an item was rumored to be found.
3.  **Consult the Community** — Every item page features a "Pilot's Log" section. Here, verified users share their loadout contexts, successful (and failed) crafting attempts, and performance reviews after the latest balance patch.
4.  **Contribute Your Finds** — For those who discover a new stat combination or a more efficient recycling route, the "Submit Intel" button allows you to propose an entry for community review.

> **Pro Tip** — Bookmark the "Recycling Hydra" page. This specific view shows the full breakdown tree of any item, showing all potential components that can be extracted, visually represented as branching paths.

## 🔧 Contributing to the Forge

LumenForge thrives on the collective effort of the community. Whether you are a data miner, a UI/UX enthusiast, or a grammar hawk for our multilingual support team, there is a place for you here.

### 🤝 The Pillars of Contribution

- **Data Verification** — Review recent submissions and mark items as "Verified" or "Contested" based on your in-game testing. This is the highest-value contribution.
- **Translation Parity** — Ensure that the nuance of item names and crafting steps is correctly translated, maintaining flavor across languages.
- **Feature Development** — If you hold coding skills, see our "Good First Issue" tags on the public repository. We welcome pull requests for bug fixes and minor widget additions.
- **Resource Curation** — Help us gather high-quality screenshots or video clips showcasing specific crafting outcomes or weapon recoil patterns.

### 📋 Submission Guidelines

- Ensure all statistics are posted without speculation. If you are guessing, mark the entry as "Theory."
- Provide clear, high-resolution screenshots as evidence for visual changes.
- Respect the code of conduct: be kind, patronizing-free, and constructive in comments.

## 🛡️ Security & Data Privacy

We hold the integrity of user-submitted data and the privacy of our community in high regard. LumenForge is designed with a zero-tracking policy on our read-only pages. User account information is minimal (username, password hash) and is used strictly for community moderation functions. We employ HTTPS encryption for all data transmissions and conduct routine security audits to ensure the platform remains safe.
